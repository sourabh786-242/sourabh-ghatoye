FAQ / Clear answers

Questions before the next step.

A transparent working relationship starts with realistic expectations and shared goals.

What does your SEO process include?

The SEO process can include technical audits, crawl and indexation reviews, on-page optimization, keyword and competitor research, internal linking, structured data review, and off-page opportunity analysis. Priorities depend on the website and its goals.

What are the seven phases of your social media workflow?

The seven phases are audience research, competitor research, social audits, content strategy, organic content and engagement, campaign planning and activation, and analytics reporting with ongoing optimization.

How do you measure marketing progress?

Progress is measured against agreed objectives using relevant analytics. Depending on the work, these may include search visibility, qualified visits, engagement, clicks, leads, and conversion signals. Results are reported only when verified in the available data.

Can you guarantee rankings, leads, or revenue?

No. Search rankings, leads, and revenue depend on many factors, so specific outcomes cannot be guaranteed. The focus is on thoughtful strategy, sound implementation, and transparent measurement.
