About me / The person behind the process

Thoughtful marketing. Grounded in evidence.

I'm Saurabh Ghatoye, a digital marketing professional focused on SEO and social media marketing. My journey is built around learning the craft, applying it to practical work, and documenting what the evidence shows.

SEO is my primary focus: understanding how websites are discovered, finding barriers in their technical foundations, and improving pages so they can better serve both people and search engines. Social media marketing complements that work by helping a brand communicate consistently and understand its audience.

I believe sustainable growth starts with useful work—not shortcuts or unsupported promises. Research sets direction; careful implementation turns plans into real changes; measurement helps show what to refine next. Each recommendation should have a reason, an owner, and a way to evaluate it.

How I think about growth

Clear thinking, careful execution, measurable learning.

01 / Understand

Start with the audience

Search behavior and social engagement are signals of real questions and needs. Good strategy begins by listening and researching before choosing tactics.

02 / Build

Strengthen the foundations

A clear site structure, useful pages, consistent messaging, and considered publishing habits make it easier for a brand to earn attention over time.

03 / Improve

Learn from real evidence

Analytics help describe what happened, not promise what comes next. I use measurement to review work, ask better questions, and guide iteration.
